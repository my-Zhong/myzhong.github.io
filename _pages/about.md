I am currently a Master student in **Applied Statistics** at **Xi'an Jiaotong University**, advised by Prof. [Jianyong Sun](https://gr.xjtu.edu.cn/jy.sun/).

I received a Bachelor of Science degree in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics, and a Bachelor of Management degree in Big Data Management and Application from Xi'an Jiaotong University.

My current research focuses on **LLM-based Automated Heuristic/Algorithm Design (LLM-based AHD/AAD)** under the supervision of Prof. [Jialong Shi](https://faculty.xjtu.edu.cn/jialong.shi/). Previously, I worked on **Neural Combinatorial Optimization (NCO)** under the supervision of Prof. [Zhenkun Wang](https://faculty.sustech.edu.cn/wangzk3/).
